THE BASICS

Four pillars of family relationship wellness

Strong relationships are not conflict-free. They make room for honesty, repair, individuality, and care.

💬

Communication

Share what you need without attacking. Ask questions, listen fully, and avoid assuming you know what someone means.

🧭

Boundaries

Healthy closeness includes limits. Boundaries help protect time, privacy, energy, and respect.

🤝

Repair

Misunderstandings happen. Owning your part, apologizing sincerely, and changing behavior can rebuild trust.

TRY THIS

A calmer way to talk through tension

When a conversation becomes stressful, use short statements that describe your experience instead of blaming the other person.

1. Name the situation“When plans change at the last minute…”
2. Name your feeling“…I feel stressed and left out of the decision.”
3. State your need“I need more notice when possible.”
4. Make a clear request“Could we text each other before changing the plan?”

BOUNDARIES

You can care about someone and still set limits.

A boundary explains what you will or will not participate in. It is clearest when it focuses on your own actions rather than controlling another person.

Time
“I can talk for 20 minutes tonight, but after that I need to rest.”
Conflict
“I want to discuss this, but I’m going to step away if we start yelling.”
Privacy
“I’m not comfortable sharing that information. I’ll tell you if I decide I’m ready.”
